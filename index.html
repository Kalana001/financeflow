Data Safety & Play Store Compliance

FinanceFlow operates on a zero-file-storage architecture. Your transaction history, custom category markers, and target saving plans are saved directly in an encrypted Supabase database protected by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS). We collect only mandatory registry variables (Email, User profile preferences).
